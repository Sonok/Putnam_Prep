\begin{problem}
A sequence is defined by $a_1 = 1$, $a_2 = 3$, and
\[ a_{n+1} = 3a_n - 2a_{n-1} \qquad \text{for all } n \ge 2. \]
Find a formula for $a_n$ in terms of $n$, and prove that it is correct for every positive integer $n$.
\end{problem}
